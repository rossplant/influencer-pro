# Plantr Collab

Plantr is the company. Plantr Collab is the product: brand–creator collaboration for any category. There is no starting vertical. It is aimed at small-to-mid Shopify sellers and nano and micro creators.

Sellers bring product records. Plantr Collab stores category, subcategory, price, currency, tags, free-form attributes, and claims with a source on each claim. Creators bring niches they will talk about, platforms, follower band, and generic exclusions (`restrictions`, `excluded_categories`, `excluded_claims`).

Notion is not the database. Postgres is the system of record when `DATABASE_URL` is set. Pricing is in `docs/Pricing.md`. Event names are in `docs/event_names.md`.

Older copies of a plant-specific overview remain in `docs/archive/` and are not the current description.
